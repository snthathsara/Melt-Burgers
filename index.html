Every burger begins with smash-seared patties, molten cheese, and genuine culinary passion.

Located on vibrant Waidya Road in Dehiwala and in Colombo 03, MELT is where burger dreams come alive. From our classic smash doubles and monster carnival stacks to loaded slutty chicken fries and Lotus Biscoff thick shakes, we deliver pure flavor that melts in your mouth.
